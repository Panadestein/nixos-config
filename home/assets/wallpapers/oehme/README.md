# Ernst Ferdinand Oehme wallpapers

These are high-resolution scans of public-domain paintings by the German
Romantic artist Ernst Ferdinand Oehme (1797–1855). The desktop palette in
`home/theme.nix` draws on their nocturnal Prussian blues, mountain mist,
lantern gold, and forest greens.

| File | Painting | Year | Resolution | Collection |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | --- |

| `02-prozession-im-nebel.jpg` | *Prozession im Nebel* | 1828 | 3071 × 2356 | Galerie Neue Meister, Dresden |
| `03-bergkapelle-im-winter.jpg` | *Bergkapelle im Winter* | 1842 | 3500 × 2467 | Städel Museum, Frankfurt am Main |
| `04-burg-hohnstein.jpg` | *Burg Hohnstein in der Sächsischen Schweiz* | 1827 | 4155 × 2951 | — |
| `05-landschaft-bei-maxen.jpg` | *Landschaft bei Maxen mit Blick auf das Elbsandsteingebirge* | 1838 | 5565 × 4085 | Kunstsammlungen Chemnitz / Wikimedia Commons |
| `06-gewittersturm-kaditz.jpg` | *Gewittersturm in der Gegend des Dorfes Kaditz* | — | 2000 × 1392 | — |

The files are retained at their source resolution and are intended to be used
through the declarative Hyprpaper configuration or the Waypaper picker.
