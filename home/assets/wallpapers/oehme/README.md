# Ernst Ferdinand Oehme wallpapers

These are high-resolution scans of public-domain paintings by the German
Romantic artist Ernst Ferdinand Oehme (1797–1855), plus one work attributed
to his circle (*Umkreis*). The desktop palette in `home/theme.nix` draws on
their nocturnal Prussian blues, mountain mist, lantern gold, and forest greens.
